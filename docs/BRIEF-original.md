# IMAX · grupoimax.mx

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. Max asked for "lo más increíble", said the first rebuild had "no animaciones, ni videos, ni nada de motion", told me to study motionsites.ai in depth, and asked me to do the work rather than ask. Everything below marked (evidence) comes from him or from the files; everything else is an authored decision.

## Evidence

- Business (Max, verbatim sense): inmobiliaria de naves industriales, venta y renta de naves y de parques industriales; constructora de naves y de parques industriales completos. "Inmobiliaria, desarrolladora y constructora de naves y parques industriales."
- Domain grupoimax.mx, already live on GitHub Pages.
- No logo, palette, photography, location, inventory, phone or figures supplied. So: no counters, no invented stats, no named parks, contact shown as "Por definir".
- All imagery is generated and labelled "Imagen ilustrativa".

## The eight topics

1. Vibe: blue hour, sodium light, wet concrete, engineered, calm weight. References: Monolith Hero and Vectrus Energy on MotionSites (cinematic video ground, restrained type); an architect's elevation drawing; a construction time-lapse.
2. Journey: the finished nave seen through the name, what IMAX does, the three ways to work with them, a nave built from bare land under the visitor's hand, a whole park toured, what the work includes, the form.
3. Energy: loud open, calm lateral middle, the build is the loudest and longest, the park is a steady glide, quiet list, still close.
4. Feeling and the one moment: see the curve and the peak below.
5. The thing no other site does: the architect's elevation draws itself over an empty lot, registered to the photograph, and the building rises inside its own drawing until the dock lights come on.
6. Range: premium, cinematic, industrial. Not minimal: dense with motion, one accent.
7. Distinct scenes, not one unbroken world (a worldflight chain was out of budget and the brief is not literally one journey).
8. Assets owned: none. Fully generated world.

## Feeling curve

1. Awe, then orientation. The name is cut out of the dark and the nave at blue hour is alive inside the letters; scrolling flies through them into the yard and the page says what IMAX is.
2. Clarity. Four options travel sideways, each one a place, each one labelled in plain words.
3. Wonder and agency (the peak). An empty lot with survey stakes; the elevation draws itself; the steel rises inside the lines; walls and roof close; the dock lights switch on.
4. Scale and confidence. The camera pulls back to a whole park and glides from the gate to the offices.
5. Reassurance. A plain list of what the work includes.
6. Resolve. A form over a still interior, the drawing finished in the footer.

No two neighbours share a feeling. Act 2 is the quiet in front of the peak.

## The peak

"I scrolled and the plan of the nave drew itself on an empty lot, then the building went up inside the lines and the lights came on." It lives in act 3, which has the largest span on the page and the only scrub clip.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where you draw the plan of a nave on an empty lot with your scroll and watch it get built until the dock lights come on.

## Grammar and why the other seven lost

Filmic one-shot. Chaptered editorial forbids media under type and the full-bleed open, and Max asked for video and motion. Live surface: there is no software to operate. Continuous world needs a chained flight we could not fund and the brief is not one journey. Typographic poster bans photographic ground and the product is a building. Gallery needs a real inventory, which does not exist yet. Split stage has no two-sided argument (there are three ways in). Rhythmic cutlist is a pulse, and B2B industrial property wants weight.

## Signature move

A registered elevation: an SVG technical drawing traced on the photograph's own geometry, whose strokes are driven by scroll, so the plan and the building occupy the same pixels and one becomes the other.

## Third revision (same day)

Max, verbatim, on the photoreal scrub: "Cuando me refiero a que la bodega debe estar deconstruida y construirse, no me refiero solo al techo como se ve ahí, me refiero absolutamente a toda la bodega. Que se vea de cero, de polvo, tiene que ser algo realmente…". The earlier clip kept the steel frame standing and only floated roof and walls.

Decisions:
- The peak now starts on bare graded ground. Three registered stills (same camera): the empty dusty pad, the whole nave taken apart (roof sheets, purlins, trusses, columns, wall panels on all four sides), and the finished nave. Two Kling 3 Pro clips join them: dust storm on the pad, the nave emerging out of the dust and opening into pieces; then every piece closing into the finished building.
- One master clip, 14.08 s, 338 frames: 0.4 s hold on the pad, clip 1 at 1.15x, 1.6 s hold on the exploded nave (0.5 s cross-dissolve between the two clips), clip 2 at 1.4x, 0.8 s hold on the finished nave. Stretched segments use blended frames.
- Travel map, by p: pad 0 to .03, dust and emergence .03 to .435, exploded hold .435 to .547, assembly .547 to .944, finished .944 to 1. Span raised to 5.8, still the largest on the page.
- Copy moved to a low, wide plate at the bottom left, over the yard, because the pieces float in the upper two thirds and the plate was hiding a third of the roof. Rail moved to the bottom right as a 3 by 2 grid. On phones the rail is gone and the plate carries a progress line.
- Seven beats, written as the construction sequence: terreno, terracerías, cimentación y estructura, piezas, montaje, cubierta, entrega. Nine tags name the floating parts during the hold; five on phones, with their own positions.
- Reduced motion shows the exploded still with every tag on.

Research pass (same round): values in the Ficha técnica come from published spec sheets of developers in Mexico and from standards, checked against the sources (NMX-R-046-SCFI-2015, an American Industries sheet, the MHI dock planning guide, a FINSA listing). They are labelled as references. No claim is made that IMAX buildings are class A, certified or compliant.

Tell-someone sentence, revised: it's the site where an empty lot fills with dust, a whole nave comes out of it in pieces, and puts itself together as you scroll.
